HEADLINE: Supply Chain Disruptions Force Vertex Semiconductors to Delay Helium-3 Processor Shipments
DATE: 2026-09-10
SENTIMENT: negative
Vertex Semiconductors confirmed today that shipment dates for its next-generation Helium-3 processor line have been pushed back to early 2027. The delay is attributed to unexpected logistics bottlenecks at its primary international packaging facility. This setback has sparked concerns among key smartphone manufacturers who had planned to feature the chip in their upcoming holiday lineups. Following the announcement, several institutional investors adjusted their near-term revenue projections for the chipmaker.
